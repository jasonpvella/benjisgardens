After years of quietly getting on with the work, it's time to start sharing a bit more of what we do.

The Journal is where we'll post updates from recent jobs, seasonal tips for Sydney gardens, and the occasional before-and-after from a project we're particularly proud of.

## What to expect

**Job updates.** Real gardens, real results. We'll share photos and a few notes from jobs across the Hills District, Upper North Shore, and Inner West.

**Seasonal advice.** Knowing *when* to trim a buxus hedge or aerate a lawn makes a real difference. We'll post practical tips tied to the Sydney climate calendar.

**Plant spotlights.** Buxus, lilly pilly, murraya, leighton green — each species has its own quirks. We'll break down what we've learned working with them across hundreds of Sydney properties.
